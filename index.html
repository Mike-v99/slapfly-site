How it plays

One hand, one minute

Flies land on the counter, you slap them. Every level asks for a few more than the last.

Six worlds, five kinds of fly

Toxic flies are quicker, red ones twitch and dodge, blue ones dash in bursts, and world five sends whole swarms.

Boss flies

Every tenth level a giant boss drops in with a health bar and a charge attack. Hit it during the wind-up to block the charge.

Combos and stars

Quick hits build a combo and a score multiplier up to x5. Finish fast and clean for three stars.

Power-ups

Fly Swatter clears an area, Freeze stops everything for four seconds, Fly Magnet pulls the swarm to the middle.

Nothing to sign up for

Progress saves on your phone. The game never connects to the internet and collects no data.
