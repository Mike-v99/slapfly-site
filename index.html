Slap Fly

Tap the flies before the clock runs out. Thirty levels in six kitchens, three boss flies, combos, power-ups and stars. Free on iPhone, no ads, no in-app purchases, works offline.

Support

Found a bug, or have a question or an idea? Open an issue on the Slap Fly support tracker and you will get an answer there:

github.com/Mike-v99/slapfly-site/issues

When reporting a problem, please say which iPhone model and iOS version you use and what you were doing when it happened.

Frequently asked

How do I get three stars? Finish with 45% of the time left and either no missed taps or a combo of five or more. The result screen tells you what the next star needs.

How do I reset my progress? Settings, then Reset Progress. It asks you to confirm first.

Does the game need an internet connection? No. It never connects to the internet.

Slap Fly collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
