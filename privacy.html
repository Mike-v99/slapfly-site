Privacy Policy – Slap Fly

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Slap Fly is a game for iPhone. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: nothing.

We do not collect any data

Slap Fly has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of its own. It never connects to the internet.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, best scores) and your settings (sound and vibration) in a small file on your device. That file never leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes it. "Reset progress" in Settings clears it too.

No third parties

Slap Fly includes no third-party software development kits that collect or share information. It does not use the Advertising Identifier.

Children

Slap Fly is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Permissions

Slap Fly asks for no permissions. It uses the phone's vibration motor for feedback if you leave vibration switched on; this needs no permission and sends nothing anywhere.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of this (for example, if a version adds optional advertising), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will say what changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Mike-v99/slapfly-site/issues.
